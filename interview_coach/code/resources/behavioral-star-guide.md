# Guide: The Behavioral (STAR+) Round

FDE behavioral rounds probe customer empathy, radical ownership, and
communicating under pressure.

**STAR+ structure**
- **Situation** — brief context.
- **Task** — what you owned.
- **Action** — what *you* did (not the team).
- **Result** — the measurable outcome.
- **+ Reflection** — what you learned / would do differently.

**Signals they want:** owning parts that weren't your fault; delivering bad news
credibly; holding a boundary with a stakeholder while keeping the relationship.
